Round Duct — Minimum Gauge (not auto-computed — see Tab 2 Round Duct manual gauge selection)
Cross-checked across 2+ secondary sources (not the primary SMACNA manual): 28ga minimum is commonly cited for 4–14" diameter on both spiral and longitudinal seam construction; spiral (lockseam) duct can commonly use 26ga up to ~24" diameter (spiral seams add stiffness — max D/t ratio ~1800 vs ~800 for longitudinal); longitudinal-seam duct is commonly capped at 26ga up to ~18" diameter. These figures are directional, not a complete verified table — the app does not auto-select round duct gauge; pick manually per run in Tab 2 and confirm against your SMACNA 3rd Ed. manual (or manufacturer's duct schedule) before fabrication.

Construction terminology (general reference, not a numeric table)
Seams — the longitudinal joint closing a duct section into a tube shape (e.g. Pittsburgh lock: a folded mechanical interlock along the seam).
Joints — the transverse connection between duct sections (e.g. S-slip and drive-slip for low pressure; standing seam for heavier/higher-pressure duct).
Reinforcement — stiffening added to large flat duct faces to control panel deflection (e.g. cross-breaking, angle-iron bracing) — required more as duct size/pressure class increases.
Connectors — hardware joining duct sections or duct-to-equipment (slip connectors, companion angles/flanges).
Flanges — bolted duct-to-duct or duct-to-equipment connections, typically at equipment tie-ins or where sections must be demountable.
This is general descriptive orientation, not a substitute for the applicable SMACNA construction table — verify exact spacing/reinforcement requirements against your SMACNA 3rd Ed. manual.
