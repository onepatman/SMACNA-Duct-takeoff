1 DUCT IDENTIFICATION & GEOMETRY
Enter Width and Depth in millimeters and Length in meters for each duct run. Gauge, materials, and hangers compute automatically per SMACNA Table 1-7 and Table 4-1.
Core formulas used per run:
Perimeter (m) = 2 × (Width÷1000 + Depth÷1000)  |  Area (sq m) = Perimeter × Length
Gauge = SMACNA T1-7 bracket of MAX(Width, Depth)  |  Hangers = ROUNDUP(Length ÷ 2.4 + 1, 0)
